Software providers have less room than ever to assume merchants will stay put. For the second year in a row, more than 60% of merchants say they have an appetite for switching platforms. In 2025, that figure stood at 62%, up from 55% in 2023, according to the 2026 Merchant Insider Report , which surveyed over 1,500 businesses across Australia, the UK and the US.
